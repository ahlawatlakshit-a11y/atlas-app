How it works

1 · Recruiter posts a role

AI generates the job description from a 1-line brief. Posted to ATLAS network, WhatsApp groups, and 70+ village kirana referral nodes.

2 · Worker calls or scans QR

Picks language. Voice agent asks 5 simple questions — age, experience, distance, lifting capacity, shift availability.

3 · AI scores & schedules

Real-time scoring against role criteria. Qualifying candidates self-book interview slots in the same call.

4 · Offer letter auto-generated

After in-person interview, recruiter clicks once. ATLAS generates and sends a localised offer letter via WhatsApp.
